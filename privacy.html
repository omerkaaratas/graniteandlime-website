Privacy

How we handle enquiry information

This notice explains what happens to the details you give when you contact Granite & Lime about painting or decorating work.

Who is responsible

Granite & Lime, [legal name of the business, for example Omer Karatas trading as Granite & Lime], Dublin, Ireland, is the controller of your enquiry information. Contact: omer@graniteandlime.ie or 083 427 7556.

What we collect and why

  • Your name, area or neighbourhood, the work you describe, and the phone number and/or email address you choose to give.
  • If you arrived from an online advertisement, the campaign name and source shown in the page address (for example "google"). This is not linked to any advertising profile.

We use this only to reply to your enquiry, discuss the work, arrange a site visit where suitable, and prepare a quotation. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.

Who processes it for us

  • [Form service, if approved — for example Formspark (Trampoline Software SRL, Belgium), which receives the form and emails it to us; data hosted in the European Economic Area.]
  • Microsoft 365, which hosts our business email.
  • If you choose WhatsApp or your own email app, that service handles your message under its own terms.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How long we keep it

[Proposed:] Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 12 months. If you become a customer, records needed for quotations, invoices and tax are kept for 6 years, as Irish tax rules require. Copies held by the form service are deleted [within 30 days] of receipt.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to its use, by contacting us at the address above. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).
