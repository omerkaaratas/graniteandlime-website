Who you deal with

One person accountable for the whole job.

Granite & Lime is run by Omer Karatas. Omer handles your enquiry, the site visit, the quote, the coordination of the work, the updates along the way and the final walk-round.

The work itself is carried out by specialist trades he works with directly: painters, tilers, plumbers, electricians and carpenters.

  • A clear answer earlyIf a job isn't right for us, we say so at the start.
  • A realistic start dateGiven with the quote, based on the actual schedule.
  • No surprise extrasNothing added to the bill without your agreement.
  • Your home respectedFloors and furniture protected, tidy at the end of each day.

How it works

Five steps, and you know where you are at each one.

  1. Form, phone or photos on WhatsApp. We confirm quickly whether it's work we take on.

  2. We look, measure and ask what you want the finished result to be.

  3. Itemised: what's included, what isn't, and what the price covers. A realistic start date comes with it.

  4. Agreed start date, trades booked in the right order, regular updates. Anything unexpected is priced and agreed before it's done.

  5. We go through it together and fix any snags before sign-off.

In writing

What a Granite & Lime quote looks like.

Unclear quotes are where most renovation disagreements start. Ours list the work line by line.

  • Preparation is listed, not assumed
  • What's included is clear, line by line
  • Exclusions are written down
  • Anything unexpected is priced and agreed before it's done

Questions

Before you get in touch.

Can you give me a price over the phone?

Photos are often enough for a rough range. A fixed price needs a site visit, so the quote reflects your actual rooms, surfaces and condition.

Who actually does the work?

Specialist trades that Omer works with directly. Omer manages the job, checks the work and stays your contact throughout.

What if something extra comes up?

If opening up a wall or floor reveals a problem, we stop, show you, explain the options and price them. Nothing extra goes ahead until you agree.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the job and the schedule. You'll get a realistic start date with your quote.

Which areas do you cover?

Homes across Dublin. If you're just outside the county, ask and we'll tell you straight away.
