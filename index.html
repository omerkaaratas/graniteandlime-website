Questions

Useful to know before you call.

Can I send photos before anyone visits?

Yes. Photos on WhatsApp help us understand the rooms. We may still need to see the rooms before giving a price.

Do I need to choose colours first?

No. You can get in touch before you have decided. Colours and finishes are confirmed as part of agreeing the work.

What preparation is involved?

It depends on the condition of the walls, ceilings and woodwork. The preparation needed for your rooms, such as filling and sanding, is discussed with you and set out in the quotation.

What about furniture and floors?

Tell us what is in the rooms. How furniture will be moved and floors covered is agreed with you before work starts.

Will I get the quotation in writing?

Yes. Where we can take the work on, the quotation is written and sets out what is included.

Do you take on smaller jobs?

Tell us about the rooms and timing, and we will let you know honestly whether it is a good fit.
