Questions

A few things worth knowing.

Can I send photographs before a site visit?

Yes. Send initial photos on WhatsApp to help explain your project. A quotation may still require a site visit and more information.

Do you provide written quotations?

Our intended process is to scope the work first and then provide a written quotation where the project can be taken forward.

Do you take on smaller painting jobs?

Tell us about the rooms, surfaces and timing. We can then discuss whether the scope is a suitable fit.
